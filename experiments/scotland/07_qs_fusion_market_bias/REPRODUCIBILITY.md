## What was compared

`r07_reproduce.jl` is wave 2's script on the wave-3 queue. The plan was frozen in DECISIONS_LOG before any fit.

**Inputs and checks:**
- It loads the saved fit by its final UUID (`results/phase3/RUNS.csv`) and checks that its model and sampler equal the candidate's.
- It rebuilds the frozen fold inputs and runs the filtration check.
- It samples fold 1 through the unchanged `Wave3Grid.sample_pending!` queue: the W2 sampler, 4 × (500 + 1000), with per-chain seeds `chain_seed(arm, 1, 0, c)`. The checkpoints are fresh scratch ones under `/root/BF_runs/qs_experiment_w3_out/phase5/<arm>/`.
- It assembles the fit after `Random.seed!(post_seed(arm, 0))`.

**Comparison:**
- Chain parameter names must be equal.
- Two channels are compared as raw Float64 bytes with SHA256: the posterior arrays (`Array(chain, append_chains = false)`) and the home/away latent arrays aligned on match id.
- To pass, the bytes must be identical and the max absolute gap 0.
- This is equality of the scientific payload. It is not equality of the whole serialised Fit, which carries timestamps.

## Prespecified verdict

Use **10a** (full-panel posterior) for (1) innovation quality/style alignment (R6 `corr_delta_q_s` 90% interval includes zero, or |median| <0.3), (2) quality dominance (C0 innovation SD ratio `r=sigma_s/sigma_q` 90% upper bound <0.6). Use **10b** (fit on preceding seasons only) for (3) prediction: the C0−R6 honest paired marginal log-density difference must not fall more than **2 fixture SE** below zero. This is the sum of *supremacy and level marginal* densities, **not** a joint density. Forecasts precede each entire week's update; earlier test-season weeks may inform later ones. “Suitable” means all three conditions pass; “unsuitable” means an alignment failure or an r interval wholly above 0.6; otherwise “inconclusive.” These are fixed diagnostic classifications, not significance tests or proof of transportability. The authoritative machine-readable thresholds and 10b sensitivity flags are in [`verdict.csv`](verdict.csv), generated by `r06_qs_report.jl`/`l06_qs_report.jl`.

## Independent checks and caveats

- **Synthetic C0 recovery** used 3 predeclared generation seeds per league for English Premier (sharp) and Scottish League Two (thin), with the same panel geometry and 4×(2000 warmup + 3000 samples). Of 18 parameter-by-seed 90% intervals, **17 cover their simulated truth**; Scottish League Two seed 3963 `sigma_q` misses (truth ≈0.02999, upper interval ≈0.02879). That miss is preserved in [`c0_recovery.csv`](c0_recovery.csv), not rerun or suppressed. These simulations test limited identifiability, not the truth of the football model.
- **Goal check** is a separate exploratory 10b paired *double-Poisson* goal log score. Across 3,053 honest fixtures, full minus no-style is +0.0067 (fixture SE 0.0023) and full minus no-quality is +0.0625 (0.0068); pooled total-goal calibration slope is 0.780 (model-based SE 0.105). League heterogeneity matters: full minus no-style is +0.0502 (0.0177) in Finland but **−0.0198 (0.0083)** in the Scottish Premiership. See [`goal_check.csv`](goal_check.csv) and the unpooled fixture rows in [`goal_fixtures.csv`](goal_fixtures.csv). No betting outcome is inferred.
- **Price/population selection:** only close books passing the unmodified completeness, overround and inversion gates enter the rate panel; the structural ladder further requires ≥5 selections. E.g., 78 accepted Scottish League Two 1X2-only books are excluded from its 615-fixture structural panel. Scottish Championship 25/26 was excluded for missing Betfair close coverage; Scottish Lower 25/26 remains. Read raw-close counts and refusal reasons in [`coverage_by_league.csv`](coverage_by_league.csv); missing or thin books may be non-random. The fixture SE does **not** adjust for serial dependence, correlated opponents or league selection.
- **Reproduction:** measured six-fit wall over all leagues exceeds 3 hours. The prescribed fresh-REPL, fixed-seed refits of English Premier and Scottish League Two yielded byte-identical posterior-derived CSVs in a **separate** output directory; timings are in [`reproduction/refit_timing.csv`](reproduction/refit_timing.csv). A further fresh-REPL regeneration from all 66 original saved chains compared every posterior-derived scientific CSV byte-for-byte and reached `R06_REGENERATE_DONE` (beast log `/root/BF_runs/logs/market_model_qs/regenerate.log`). Original draws and canonical results were not overwritten. See [`HANDOVER.md`](HANDOVER.md) for source/pane and test details.

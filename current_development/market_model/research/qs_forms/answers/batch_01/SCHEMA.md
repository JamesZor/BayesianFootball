# Batch 01 conventions and availability

- R01: all retained physical theta draws (3,000 × 4 chains/fit), weekly innovation variances in squared log-rate units. Axial angles in degrees, wrapped to [−90,90); tilt distance is `abs(delta_deg)`. `I` is dependence information, never forecast gain. Summary probabilities repeat per quantity.
- R02: 10a, 128 deterministic chain-balanced linked theta draws; one exact joint FFBS path each. `draw` is selected-index ordinal; manifest gives original chain/draw. Season averages cover every calendar slot from first through last accepted fixture, reproducing the published convention. Full-roster and fixed active-season-cohort gauges are separate. `n_missing`/`reason` report undefined rank-one shortcuts rather than silently dropping them; quantiles use available values. Partial panels and roster membership are in `season_windows.csv`.
- R03: 10b, D=log(lambda_h)−log(lambda_a), M=(log(lambda_h)+log(lambda_a))/2. The transformation determinant is one; no density constant changes. All forecasts precede every update of their week. Marginal published reproduction uses median theta, new scores use the 128-component Gaussian mixture. Week-block intervals are distinct from posterior intervals. Original lag-pair rows are resampled by their later week; no artificial cross-block neighbors. Team lag is appearance-lag one within season. Venue contrast is home-minus-away *team proxy*: D changes sign in the away role, M does not; contrast/bootstrap_se is the standardized distribution.
- R04–R06 point screens: 10a median linked theta, fixed active-season-cohort centering. The conditional null keeps the actual first eight **observed** weeks unchanged, draws their joint filtered endpoint, and advances through all subsequent calendar slots with actual engine Q/R and fixture missingness. Exactly 99 shared panels/sentinel; the suffix only is compared. R6 sensitivity has no additional null. Null rank=(1+count(null≤observed))/100 is a plug-in predictive rank, not a p-value. Non-sentinel/full-season/R6 null fields are unavailable, not zero.
- Steps: both endpoints must be strictly after the eight-observed-week cutoff; conservative eligibility requires an appearance in every compared calendar slot; this excludes byes/missing books as well as genuine inactive gaps. Counts/exclusions are explicit. Gaps are consecutive appearances separated by >1 slot, separately labelled; no managerial/transfer labels. Energy divides squared motion by the scheduled weekly axis variance. Off-diagonal co-motion reports both the auxiliary weekly mean pairwise centered-step product and actual temporal sample covariance for each team pair/season with ≥3 jointly eligible steps (equal weight per pair-season). Neither demonstrates common quality shocks. Team energy CV/rank statistics pool team-season summaries, not independent teams over time.
- R06: q is centered/scaled by season sample SD; s remains in log-rate units. Skewness/kurtosis use population central moments. LOTO error changes are quadratic minus comparator **per team** (negative is better). Full-season FFBS uncertainty is not a null interval. Tier labels align the single largest gap between adjacent seasons; no gap search for persistence. <8-team or empty-suffix windows are unavailable.
- R07: unchanged C0 10b states; deletion variants are not refits. Plug-in uses median linked theta. Mixture methods reuse 128 theta draws × first two/all four joint state samples per week across fixtures/variants. Allocation score is mixture joint minus mixture total, exactly. Integrated frozen-style is explicitly omitted without a joint frozen/current conditional construction. Its plug-in uses the training-end filtered state, including the training-conditioned entry prior of never-directly-observed roster teams. MC_UNSTABLE flags delta changes >0.001 nats/fixture, without extra samples.
- R08: finite-league posterior uncertainty uses 12,000 fixed-seed independent permutations, preserving matched r/sigma_obs within each league. Overround is a fixture-count-weighted mean of saved season medians, not a raw pooled median. Archive trade-price timestamps/history exist; executable quote age/update events, depth/spread and independently verified stable team IDs are not supplied by these snapshots. `start_spread` is optimizer-start dispersion. Unverified names are not fuzzy-joined into movers.
- Forecast blocks: 999 noncircular moving-block samples of 8 observed weeks, within league-season, retaining all fixtures. Headline score comparisons also use 4/12 weeks. Pooled fixture weighting uses original league fixture counts; equal-league weighting uses 1/11. Normalized probabilities/ratios are dimensionless; scores natural-log nats; rates and s are natural-log goal-rate units; steps calendar weeks.
- Large tables/caches stay on the beast. `manifest.csv` records exact paths, SHA256s, row counts/columns and seeds; only tables ≤5 MB are committed. Blank unavailable values must be interpreted with method/window/status and the limitations above, never as numerical zeros.
